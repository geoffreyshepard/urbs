# Art direction test: shared brief

Three renderings of the same six buildings, so the comparison is about the art
method, not the design. Every option follows these readability rules:

1. **Category accent colour.** Each category owns one accent that no other
   category uses on roofs, awnings or banners.
   | Category | Accent |
   |---|---|
   | Housing | none: muted plaster and faded terracotta (housing is the background) |
   | Food | saffron-yellow awning, warm ochre |
   | Industry | charcoal-slate roof, orange forge glow |
   | Storage | dark timber, tan stone, big doors |
   | Religion | white marble, gold trim |
2. **Function props on the lot.** A building shows what it does outside its walls.
3. **Category ground.** Housing sits on garden/grass, food and industry on packed
   dirt yards, storage on a paved loading yard, religion on marble paving.
4. **Size and silhouette hierarchy.** Civic buildings are taller and lighter;
   houses are low and quiet.

| Id | Footprint | Design |
|---|---|---|
