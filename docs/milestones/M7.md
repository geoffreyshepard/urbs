# M7 — Society

## Delivered
- All six classes simulated: patricians (estate income, wealth tax, buy domestic slaves), equites (work as clerks, teachers, physicians), plebeians, liberti, slaves, soldiers (M9).
- Lifecycle in the daily pass: age-dependent mortality (lifespan and sickness shift it); marriage (men initiate, same social stratum, age gap at most 15; a new household if a house has room, otherwise the wife joins his); births (married women 16–42, scaled by happiness, room at home and the family-minded trait); inheritance (heirless estates pass to the city).
- Traits (`data/defs/traits.json`, 15 traits, opposites never combine, children inherit about 30% of the time) modify need rates, work rate, skill growth, crime, health, lifespan, fertility and mood.
- Skills per sector grow with each worked shift (cap raised by schooling); a building's output scales from 75% to 125% with its present workers' skill.
- Life goals (better home, marriage, savings, family, freedom, prestige) give a temporary happiness bonus when reached; ambitious citizens get frustrated by unmet goals.
- Slavery:
  - Private slaves are bought by wealthy elite households.
  - Public slaves are bought by the city and housed in slave quarters; they draw no wages.
  - Treatment policy changes slave happiness and escape risk.
  - Manumission policy plus years of service frees slaves into liberti, who set up their own plebeian household.
- Crime: idle, poor, unhappy (and brawling or drunk) citizens steal from neighbours. Guard coverage cuts it by 70%. The city crime rate lowers everyone's sense of safety.
- Riots: low average morale plus a trigger (hunger above 25% or plebeian taxes above 15%) can start a riot at a civic building or market. It damages the building and loots the treasury unless guards cover it.
- Inspector: traits, life goal, family links, skills, memory log, full happiness breakdown. Finance panel: slave treatment, manumission, buy public slaves.

## Exit check
`tests/scenarios/generations_20y.json` (20 game years, about 3 s): 300+ births and 846 married citizens. The population settles around 1,100–1,400. With manumission encouraged, the 20 public slaves become liberti over time. Patricians and equites settle in the domus plots.
