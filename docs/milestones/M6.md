# M6 — Services and housing evolution

## Delivered
- Desirability field (`map.desir`): buildings radiate their `[value, range]` with linear falloff; houses add by tier (fine houses help, crowded insulae and tents don't); terrain adds (water views, woods; marsh hurts). Recomputed when buildings change. Houses sample the average over their footprint + ring daily; it gates evolution and gives the elite a prestige happiness term.
- Water network:
  - Aqueducts (painted lines, passable underneath) carry water from open water or a full reservoir, and water never flows uphill (height must not rise along the flow).
  - Reservoirs fill from a wet aqueduct or adjacent water.
  - Pipes (underground layer) are pressurised from full reservoirs.
  - Fountains and thermae work only with piped water; houses next to a pressurised pipe count as piped (villa requirement).
- Sewers (underground layer) drain when connected to open water; houses next to a draining sewer count as connected (large insula requirement).
- Wells keep working anywhere; groundwater shows in an overlay.
- Education: children (6–13) attend school in the morning if one is in range; equites and patricians have an education need served by libraries and academies.
- Patrician line: equites and patrician households immigrate into domus plots when the city is pleasant (average happiness ≥ 115); patricians live off estate income and pay the wealth tax; elite happiness includes education and prestige (desirability).
- Overlays (O to cycle): desirability, water access, pipes & sewers & wet aqueducts, coverage for any service by walking time, housing level, elevation, fertility, groundwater.
- Build tools: pipes and sewers are 4-connected line tools on the underground layer; bridges are road lines over water; demolish clears underground lines.

## Exit check
Housing evolves and devolves with inspectable causes (building panel names the next missing requirement; devolution alerts name what was lost). Unit test covers aqueduct gradient, reservoir filling, pipes to a fountain, uphill failure, and sewer drainage.
