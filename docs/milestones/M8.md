# M8 — Religion, threats, seasons

## Delivered
- Gods (`data/defs/gods.json`, 13 including Isis, Serapis and Divus Augustus; scenarios choose which are worshipped). Each god's mood drifts toward a target set by dedicated temples (temple 1, great temple 3, monuments 6; the Pantheon honours all), shrines, and worship visits (offerings). Exalted gods occasionally bless (favor, births, morale, happiness, harvest, safe seas, trade prices, workshops, fireproofing, hearth, health); wrathful gods punish (lightning fire, barren season, desertion, discontent, blight, earthquake, no trade, workshop accidents, great fire, house fires, plague). God moods add a happiness term.
- Festivals: pay for a festival (or grand festival) to any god; the god's feast day doubles the effect; everyone gets a few days of happiness bonus.
- Seasons and weather: daily weather by season (rain, heat, storm, snow); heat spells can become drought; storms damage roofs and can sink docked ships; winter stops farms. Day/night and season tint the world, with rain and snow effects.
- Fire: risk builds hourly from the building's fire risk, crowded insulae, summer, heat and drought (rain and blessings reduce it), and vigiles in range reset it. Burning buildings spread fire to neighbours, are fought by vigiles (and rain), and become ruins if they burn out.
- Collapse: structural wear for risky buildings and tall insulae; engineers in range repair it.
- Disease: an outbreak chance per house rises with a bad neighbourhood, crowding, insulae without sewers, and no fountain water; physicians cut it. Infected houses make residents sick and spread to neighbours. Sick citizens seek a physician or hospital and recover over time; sickness raises mortality.
- Servile revolt: when slaves are 15%+ of the population and miserable, they torch workplaces and flee; guards catch some.
- Disasters (triggered by gods, scenarios or debug): flood (low ground near water), earthquake, drought, storm, blight.
- Ruins: occupants lose home or job, inventories are lost; rubble stays until demolished.
- UI: religion panel (moods, festivals), threats debug panel (trigger any threat at the selected building), temple dedication in the build menu, hazard info in the building panel.

## Exit check
`tests/scenarios/threats.json` triggers every threat (fire, plague, storm, drought, earthquake, collapse, riot, revolt, blight, flood, wrath, blessing). With vigiles, engineers, physicians and guards in place the town survives (population above 550, no more than 25 ruins). Every threat can also be triggered in-game from the threats panel.
