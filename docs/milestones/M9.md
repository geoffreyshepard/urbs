# M9 — Military

## Delivered
- Unit types in data (`data/defs/units.json`): militia, legionary cohort, auxiliary archers and cavalry, ballistae; enemies: barbarian warbands, skirmishers and horsemen, Punic infantry, rebel slaves.
- Recruitment at a barracks or fort needs gear in its storage (weapons, armor, planks for ballistae; porters deliver it like any other demand) and recruits: men aged 17–45 from the plebs and liberti, unemployed only by default, or employed too under conscription. Recruits leave the workforce and their household. Disbanded veterans settle in vacant housing.
- Upkeep: daily wages per soldier (military ledger) and food from the home barracks; hungry units lose morale. Training grounds near a holding unit raise its experience faster.
- Soldiers are a separate struct-of-arrays pool that ticks every tick while deployed. Units (cohorts) take orders (hold, move, return, campaign) and fight in formations (line, wedge, testudo, loose) with a stance (aggressive, defensive).
- Movement: units march along cached flow fields (8-dir Dijkstra, up to 16 cached, invalidated when the map changes). Soldiers keep formation slots around the moving anchor and use a spatial hash (4×4-tile cells, rebuilt each tick without allocation) for separation and targeting. Aggressive holding units advance on nearby enemies.
- Combat: melee and ranged attacks (pooled projectiles). Damage scales with attack, experience and morale, and is reduced by defense; drilled formations get +30% defense, testudo triples it against missiles, and a cavalry charge multiplies the first blow.
- Morale: losses drain it, kills and calm restore it. Below 15 a unit routs: city units fall back and rally later, enemies flee the map. Mars' blessing and wrath affect morale.
- Walls block movement; gates let citizens and the city's troops through but stop enemies, who attack buildings in their way (damage, occasional fire, ruins). Staffed towers shoot at enemies within 10 tiles.
- Raids: enemy warbands spawn at a map edge and march on the heart of the city (forum or palace). Triggered by scenarios or the threats panel.
- Off-map campaigns (skirmish, campaign, conquest): the unit leaves the map for 8 days. The outcome depends on strength, experience and morale; plunder goes to the treasury, captives become public slaves, and victory earns favor.
- UI: military panel (recruitment policy, unit list, formation, stance, return, disband, campaign), raise buttons in the barracks inspector, right-click to move the selected unit.

## Exit check
`tests/scenarios/stress_battle.json`: 3,384 soldiers (42 city units vs 45 enemy units) fight in a 10,000-citizen city. Headless sim averages 0.5 ms per tick. In the release game it holds 60 FPS (vsync) at 1× with about 0.7 ms of sim per frame.
