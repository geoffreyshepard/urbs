# M11 — Campaign vertical slice (v0.1)

## Delivered
- **Front end:** a main menu with a pixel-art logo over a live city, plus settings (UI scale, edge scrolling, autosave, difficulty) and save slots (autosave every four days, quicksave, six slots).
- **Pause menu.**
- **Campaign layer:**
  - A pixel-art province map of Italy and the Mediterranean with the 16 mission sites.
  - A career profile: rank per mission, a salary paid into personal savings, and completed missions.
  - Missions unlock in order. Former cities become trade partners.
- **Sandbox setup:** map size, seed, coast, river, hills, starting money.
- **HUD:** treasury, population, date and season, speed controls, happiness and the ratings, with pixel-art icons.
- **Buttons and panels:** Build, Rome, Advisors, Finance, Goods, Trade, Labor, Religion, Military, Research, Alerts, Help; F12 opens the debug panel.
- **Roman UI skin.**
- **Build menu:** filtered by unlocks, grouped by category.
- **Civic research** (Scientia): techniques unlocked with research points from schools and libraries.
- **Missions 1–4**, each defined entirely in `data/scenarios/mXX/` (scenario.json + script.lua), with advisor tutorial steps, Rome's requests, threats and goals:
  1. **Latium:** the founding (housing, water, food, forum).
  2. **Ostia:** fish, salt, storage, the first trade route, fire.
  3. **Beneventum:** a hill frontier (quarries, temple, militia, raids).
  4. **Capua:** olive oil and wine chains, amphorae, patrician domus, theater, two requests.
- **Difficulty** (docs/02 §15) scales:
  - threats (fire, collapse, plague, drought, storms)
  - raid size
  - starting treasury
  - request penalties
- **Stockpile trade mode:** markets leave a good in storage, so you can gather Rome's requests.
- **Autoplay bot** (`sim/autoplay`): plays through Commands like a player. It runs headless (`urbs_headless --autoplay`) and in the game (`urbs --autoplay`, demo/screenshots; never autosaves).
- **Packaging:** `tools/package/package_macos.sh` builds a self-contained, ad-hoc signed `Urbs.app` (data and atlas in `Contents/Resources`) and `dist/urbs-0.1.0-macos.zip`.

## Balance changes found by playtesting with the bot
- **Labor:**
  - Hiring is round-robin within a sector priority, so a chain is never starved behind a full warehouse.
  - Construction sites hire builders only once their materials are delivered or on the way.
- **Wages:** wages are compared with Rome's going rate (4/day) and affect worker happiness.
- **Trade:** trade posts stage only goods an open route still buys, up to 80 per good. This was the city's main real income, and it had been blocked by unsellable stock.
- **Entrances and raiders:**
  - Building entrances prefer tiles connected to the map entry (no isolated pockets).
  - Raiders that cannot reach the city withdraw.
- **Content:** Etruria sells cut stone (Capua has no stone deposits).

## Exit check
- `ctest --preset long` includes `mission_m01_latium` … `mission_m04_capua`: the bot must win each mission within 160 days. It wins all four on Otium, Civitas and Imperium, in 14–110 days.
- Performance is unchanged: `stress_50k` averages about 0.5 ms per tick with 0 allocations inside `tick()`.
- A human playtest is still the real exit criterion. The bot is a lower bound on how playable the missions are, not a substitute.

## Known limitations
- Only macOS is packaged. Windows and Linux build from source with CMake presets, but are untested.
- No audio (by design, docs/09).
- Missions 5–16 exist on the province map but have no scenario data yet (M12–M13).
