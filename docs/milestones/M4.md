# M4 — Citizens' daily life

## Delivered
- Housing tiers in data (`data/defs/housing.json`): plebeian tent → hut → casa → small insula → large insula; patrician domus → grand domus → villa urbana. Each tier lists requirements (service access by walking time, food kinds, specific foods, household goods, desirability, sewer, piped water, patrols).
- Daily house check (staggered): evolve when the next tier's requirements are all met; devolve after two failed checks of the current tier (alert names the lost requirement); evicts the newest households if the house shrinks.
- Utility decisions: candidates scored from schedule + needs (sleep, thirst, hunger, shift, midday meal, shopping, water fetching, worship/bathing/entertainment in free time). Every decision stores a `Reason` shown in the inspector.
- Service visits with capacity; a visit lifts the need above the "wants to go" threshold, better services last longer. Water: households keep a water stock, fetched at the nearest well or fountain.
- Happiness: per-citizen daily recompute with a breakdown (needs centred on 150, food variety, housing tier, employment, taxes, health; gods and events hooks for later milestones).
- Immigration (hourly, daytime): city appeal from food stock, free jobs, unemployment and average happiness; households walk in from the map edge to the nearest house with room. Emigration: households unhappy for three days may leave on foot.
- Labor screen: seven sectors with player priorities (1–5) driving the allocator.
- Alerts feed with per-day deduplication; clicking one jumps to the building.
- Inspector: citizen (name, age, class, activity, why, home/work links, household, needs, happiness breakdown, follow cam) and building (state, construction materials, workers, residents, next-tier blocker, visitors, production, stock).
- Roman names from `data/defs/names.json` (praenomen + family nomen + cognomen; feminine forms; single names for slaves).

## Exit check
`tests/scenarios/town_1000.json`: a town seeded with 500 people grows past 900 in ten days through immigration; houses evolve to huts and casae. In the game, clicking any citizen shows what they are doing and why.
