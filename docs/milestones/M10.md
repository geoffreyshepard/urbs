# M10 — Governance and narrative

## Delivered
- Ratings, computed daily:
  - **Culture:** share of residents covered by worship (40%), entertainment (35%) and education (25%).
  - **Prosperity:** housing level, employment and treasury health.
  - **Security:** crime, ruins and fires, enemies against our soldiers.
  - **Favor:** see below.
- Favor with Rome (the Senate in the Republic, the Emperor in the Empire), 0–100. It moves with fulfilled or failed requests, elite happiness, debt, loans, blessings and campaign victories.
- Requests from Rome (goods, denarii, troops) with deadlines; goods are taken from storage. Success brings favor and a reward; a missed deadline costs favor.
- Loans from Rome (20% interest, repaid daily over two years); long debt erodes favor.
- Mission goals (population, ratings, requests, specific buildings, endurance). Failure on Civitas/Imperium: favor at zero, or (Imperium) debt beyond the limit. Victory and defeat windows.
- Unlocks: buildings available up to the mission number plus a scenario's extra unlocks; the build menu only shows unlocked buildings.
- Lua 5.4 scripting (sol2), sandboxed (no io/os/load/require). It runs only at event points (`on_start`, `on_day`, choice callbacks, `on_request`), with allocation explicitly allowed there. The `urbs` API covers messages, choices, population and ratings, favor, stock, giving goods, money, requests, raids, any threat trigger, unlocks, building counts, god moods and worship, trade routes, feast days, win/lose, deterministic random numbers, and logging. Persistent script state lives in `urbs.state` and is saved with the game; functions are re-created by re-running the script on load.
- Advisors (engineer, quaestor, haruspex, legate, aedile) with pixel portraits, giving plain-language reports of their domain.
- Message and choice dialogues with advisor portraits; Rome panel (ratings, requests with Send, loan, goals).
- Crisp pixel-art icons in the UI (nearest-upscaled UI texture, since the ImGui backend samples linearly).

## Exit check
`data/scenarios/m01_latium/` is a complete mission defined entirely in data (`scenario.json`) and Lua (`script.lua`): advisor tutorial steps, a request from Rome, a choice event that opens a trade route, and goals. `tests/scenarios/mission_script.json` and the unit test check the script API, choices, request failure, and that save/load round-trips script state and messages exactly.
