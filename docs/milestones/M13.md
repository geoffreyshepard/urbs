# M13 — Campaign: Empire (missions 11–16)

## Delivered
- **Missions 11–16** in data and Lua:
  11. **Lugdunum:** the Republic becomes an Empire mid-mission (`urbs.set_empire()`: the Emperor's favor, the imperial cult), with a mint and a triumphal arch.
  12. **Alexandria:** the Nile flood, the grain fleet, sand pits and glassworks, the circus, Isis and Serapis, and the lighthouse.
  13. **Colonia Agrippina:** waves of Germanic raiders across the Rhine, forts, a siege workshop and ballistae, winters.
  14. **Londinium:** Boudica's revolt burns the city on day 50; the Emperor pays for the rebuilding.
  15. **Antioch:** luxury trade with Parthia (the Silk Road), jewelers, glass and dyes, an earthquake.
  16. **Roma:** rebuild Rome after the great fire, reach 10,000 citizens, and build the Colosseum, the Pantheon and the Circus Maximus. The fiscus pays grants, and a plague strikes.
- **New mechanics:**
  - **Mint:** recipes may strike coins (`"coins"`) instead of producing goods. Silver becomes denarii in the treasury under a new ledger line, "Coins from the mint".
  - **Siege workshop:** a military building where ballistae are built (`trained_at`).
  - **Nile (`"nile": true`):** on the first day of summer the low land by the water floods. Buildings there are damaged (fields are not), and the soil is renewed; harvests then draw it down.
  - **Arid maps (`"arid"`):** desert beyond a fertile strip along the water.
  - **Parthia:** a new trade partner (land route) that sells silver, dye and jewelry.
- **Regional art:**
  - `tools/artgen/regions.py` makes `north` (slate and timber), `east` (whitewash and pale stone) and `egypt` (mudbrick and earthen roofs) variants of housing and 25 common buildings by remapping palette ramps.
  - Scenarios set `"region"`, and the renderer prefers `<sprite>@<region>`.
- **Save format v3:**
  - Adds the Mint ledger column, the Nile flag, the region, and routes matched by partner id, so new partners don't break old saves.
  - v1 and v2 saves still load.

## Fixes found on the way to 10,000 citizens
- **Errands:**
  - Households could lose their water fetcher for good: a dead or departed citizen kept the role.
  - Errand hours are now 06:00–21:00, children of ten can fetch water, and anyone can run an errand when the household has none of the good.
  - Families now shop for household goods they lack. Before, markets were full of unsold pottery and houses never reached the casa.
- **Bot:**
  - Large buildings go on open ground; monuments need only the first stage's share of materials.
  - Goal materials are never exported, and broken chains are repaired.
  - Imports food when hungry, and doesn't stockpile a food the city can't spare.
  - Keeps houses off the Nile floodplain.
  - A garrison only when threatened.
  - Wages are never cut (in a closed economy that only impoverishes the buyers).
  - Housing never freezes when no job can be created.
  - Supplies goal sites still under construction, and borrows from Rome after a disaster.
  - Clears sites that turn out cut off from the town, and blacklists the spot.
- **Nile soil:** harvests draw the soil down 1 point per batch (3 exhausted it within days); the flood renews land up to height 3.

## Exit check
- **Bot results:** the autoplay bot wins 28 of 32 runs (16 missions × 2 simulation seeds, 300 days) and has won every mission at least once.
- **Gate:** `ctest --preset long` gates missions 1–12, 14 and 16 on seed 1. Missions 13 (Colonia) and 15 (Antioch) win with some seeds; a human playtest should confirm them.
- **Remaining bot failures:** most are favor lost to missed recurring requests, or a large raid that wrecks the city before the bot can recover.

## Performance
- Mission 16 at 10,000 citizens: about 0.4–0.7 ms per tick on average, p99 about 2–5 ms.
- At 4× speed that is about 1 ms of simulation per 60 Hz frame. Ticks over 16 ms are about 0.005% of ticks (OS jitter).
