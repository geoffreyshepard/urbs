# M3 — Building and logistics

## Delivered
- Placement via commands: fixed buildings, painted areas (min/max size from defs, workers and cost scale with area), 8-direction road lines, demolish rectangles. Treasury is charged at placement.
- Physical construction: a site has an inventory for its material cost and builder jobs (2–16 by area). Porters deliver materials, builders work during work hours, the building completes when progress reaches `build_work`, and builders are released.
- Inventories per building (storage capacity, producer buffers, market stock targets) with reserved-in/out counts.
- Haul matcher (two goods per tick, each good every ~20 ticks): pass A serves active demand (construction sites, producer inputs, market targets) from the nearest supplier (storage or producer of that good); pass B clears producer outputs to the nearest accepting storage. Reservations mean no two porters chase the same goods.
- Porters are the workers of storage, market and trade buildings: they claim the best open task (distance, priority, age), walk to the source, pick up, and deliver.
- Production: an hourly event per building turns worker-ticks into recipe batches; farms scale with soil fertility and stop in winter.
- Labor allocator (hourly): fills vacancies by priority (construction > food > water/markets/storage > services > other), choosing the nearest unemployed adult of an allowed class.
- Households buy real food and goods at markets; eating consumes household stock (variety comes from distinct food slots).
- Game: build menu, placement ghost with valid/invalid tint and cost, painted drag, road and demolish tools, goods ledger window.
- Scripted scenarios: `start` (treasury, roads, prebuilt buildings with stock, households) and timed `orders`; test expectations for stock, production and active buildings.

## Exit check
`tests/scenarios/build_town.json`: a farm, mill, bakery and market get built from orders by the town's own builders and porters, and the bread chain runs (about 36 bread a day). Bread is sold through the market rather than piling up in storage, so the expectation checks production.
