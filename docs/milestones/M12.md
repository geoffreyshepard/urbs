# M12 — Campaign: Republic (missions 5–10)

## Delivered
- **Missions 5–10** in data and Lua (`data/scenarios/m05_syracuse` … `m10_corinth`), each with advisor messages, the new systems of its era, requests, threats and goals (docs/03):
  5. **Syracuse:** harbor and sea trade, grain and garum for Rome, a storm.
  6. **Carthago Nova:** mines, smelters, armory, barracks and legions; Iberian then Punic raids.
  7. **Aquileia:** reservoir, pipes, fountains, thermae, sewers, a fort; Alpine raids; winter.
  8. **Narbo:** a trade hub with a cart depot, basilica, library and great temple; a fair (choice).
  9. **Campania:** the Servile War, with slave treatment, a revolt, Spartacus's army, curia, hospital and amphitheater.
  10. **Corinth:** marble, sculptors, odeon, academy, research, an earthquake.

  After its scripted requests, Rome keeps sending new ones, so a missed request can be made up.
- **Monuments framework:**
  - Construction in four stages, each needing its share of the materials, with up to 40 builders.
  - Dedication effects in data (`dedication`): favor, a city-wide happiness bonus for some days, a pleased god, and permanent culture.
  - The building panel shows the stage and the dedication.
- **Remaining buildings now work:**
  - Cart depots: carters haul long-distance loads of 40.
  - Stables (cavalry) and forts (ballistae), via the units' `trained_at` field.
  - Basilica and curia: a city-wide crime cut while staffed; the curia also slowly gains favor.
- **Save format v2** (civic state). v1 saves still load, and `tests/saves/` holds a v0.1 save as a fixture.
- **Stockpile trade mode:** markets and workshops leave the good in storage.

## Balance pass (from autoplay playtesting)
- **Roads and commutes:** roads are fast (commutes had eaten 40% of the day); service ranges were scaled to match. Far workers skip the midday trip home.
- **Exports:** staged export goods stay at the trade post; posts stage only goods an open route still buys.
- **Fire:** spreads once per neighbour per hour, and vigiles contain it.
- **Houses and homelessness:** houses devolve after three failed checks; homeless, unhappy families emigrate after four days.
- **Prosperity:** the treasury term is measured in days of wages.
- **Debt:** the favor penalty scales with difficulty.
- **Entry roads:** they always reach the mainland, and the entry tile is kept while any house can reach it.
- **Military:** militia are a little stronger, and raiders that cannot reach the city withdraw.

## Autoplay bot
- **Economy:**
  - Plans material supply before placing, repairs broken chains, and caps stock per good, closing surplus producers.
  - Invests in exports by value and builds a harbor for sea routes.
  - Saves up for grand goal buildings and keeps a reserve of about half a day's wages.
- **City management:**
  - Plumbing: a shore reservoir, then pipes.
  - Fire watch, ruins clearing, and clearing of sites that stall for good.
  - Garrison and tactics; annona and wage policy in hard times.
- **Diagnostics:** idle reasons and an action log with `--daily --verbose`.
- **Evaluation:** `tools/bot_missions.sh [days] [difficulty]` runs every mission in parallel.

## Exit check
- The bot wins all ten missions on Otium, Civitas and Imperium within 300 days (most in 30–120 days).
- `ctest --preset long` runs missions 1–10; all long and dev tests pass.
- `stress_50k` averages about 0.5 ms per tick with 0 allocations inside `tick()`.
